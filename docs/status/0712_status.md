# Strange Ranger 0.7.12: Phase 4b, a life there

From the lab, 23 November 2026. With this, the building of Phase 4 is done: the town (4a) and a life there (4b). Next is the 4b play test. No spoilers below: the stories are only counted. Screenshots are in `docs/status/0712_shots/`.

## Heart events and romance

- **52 heart events**, at 2, 4, 6 and 8 hearts for everyone and at 10 for a sweetheart. Each is a short scene: people walk up, turn, show a feeling in a bubble over their head, talk, and sometimes ask you to choose.
- An event starts when you walk into its place, at its time of day, with the hearts reached. They come in order, and each plays once. The hours come round every day, so none can be missed for good.
- **Romance, as in Stardew, up to dating.** Wren, Teo, Nell and Sol, with a 200g bouquet from Gifts & Notions given (C) at 8 hearts. Dating lifts their cap to 10 hearts, opens a last event and their own lines, and now and then they leave a wrapped gift in the mailbox. Click a sweetheart on the People page to say "just friends"; the hearts stay. Pip is never part of romance in any way.

## Esme's readings

At her caravan, from 6 pm, Esme reads three cards for 10g, once a day.

- **The Sky** gives the weather two days ahead, and is always right.
- **The Path** draws a big, vague circle on the map round something you haven't found yet, marked with her symbol. It never points past the river.
- **The Heart** tells you something true about someone you know: a gift they'd love, a birthday coming, or where they'll be tomorrow.

If she has nothing left to tell, she reads for free.

## Festivals

On festival day the square is dressed from morning. Walk in during the festival's hours and everyone is there; time stands still until you walk out.

- **The Thaw Fair** (spring 13, morning): enter something you made, grew or found in the Grand Display.
- **Lantern Night** (summer 20, evening): choose a lantern's colour and a wish.
- **The Big Foot Supper** (autumn 18, evening): judge the look-alike contest, then eat at the long table.
- **The Long Night** (winter 25, evening): give the fire something for luck, then hear Agnes's story, a new one each year.

Each festival has a stall and its own lines for everyone. Its prize is a piece for the station. Skipping one costs nothing, and park visitors double on a festival's weekend.

## The station as a home

- **Three upgrades** are ordered from Gus at the carpentry, in order. Each takes his crew three days, and you can still sleep there.
  - **Shipshape**: the roof and shutters mended; warmer inside.
  - **A proper kitchen**: a cooking range (cook without a fire) and a 24-slot pantry for food.
  - **A second room**: twice the floor, a second window and a reading chair.
- **Decorating.** 45 pieces of furniture and decoration from Gifts & Notions and the carpentry, 3 festival prizes, 6 keepsakes, and 6 wallpapers and 6 floors.
  - Hold a piece and press C to set it down in front of you. Face the back wall to hang a picture.
  - C with an empty hand picks a piece up again.
  - The bed, chest, workbench and kitchen can be lifted and moved, but never taken out of the station.
  - A faint outline shows where the piece will go: green if it fits, red if not. Nothing can block the way from the door to anything you use.

## The garden

A fenced six by six plot behind the station.

- Dig it with the shovel and plant seeds from the outfitters by hand: three crops a season, none in winter.
- Water with the can ($50), or let the rain do it.
- A crop grows only on watered days and never dies.
- Peas, tomatoes and beans regrow.
- When the season turns, old crops wither and can be cleared.
- Your first tomato, pumpkin, or peas and radish give you the idea for a garden dish.
