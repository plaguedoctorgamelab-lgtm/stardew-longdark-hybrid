# Strange Ranger 0.6.9: town fixes

From the lab, 22 November 2026. The three things noted in 0.6.7 are fixed. Screenshots are in `docs/status/069_shots/`.

- **The creek's bends.** Where Dusk Creek steps up or down, the shore now turns a proper corner with its sand rim, instead of showing a square of grass. The lab makes these inner corners from Mark's two side tiles, since his sheet has none. The cobbles and gravel lanes get the same treatment.
- **The Percolator's windows glow at night.** Its glass is a darker blue than Art Request 19 asked for, so the game is told where its windows are. Casa Luna had the same problem the other way round: its turquoise frames glowed and the glass didn't. Now the glass glows.
- **The yard fences are bigger.** The picket fence, hedge and stone wall are half as big again, so they read clearly and lose the speckle. The hedge and wall pieces overlap a little so there are no gaps.

The Town Hall still keeps Mark's clock and flag.
