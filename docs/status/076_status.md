# Strange Ranger 0.7.5 and 0.7.6: the houses, and Townsfolk Favors

From the lab, 23 November 2026. With these, the building of Phase 4a is done: the town, its people, talking and gifts, shops, clothing, the People page and calendar, the houses and favors. Next is the 4a play test. Screenshots are in `docs/status/075_shots/` and `docs/status/076_shots/`.

## The houses (0.7.5)

Knock at a house while someone who lives there is home, between 8 am and 9 pm, and they let you in. Each house is dressed with your Art Request 21 pieces. They see you out when it gets late.

## Townsfolk Favors (0.7.6)

- **24 hand-written favors**, two for each person: Wren's rumor run, Rosa's spring onions, Teo's missing notebook, Marge's old route, Agnes's archive, Gus's hour in the workshop, Sol's sketch of the pond, Pip's ranger test, and more.
- **Five kinds of everyday favor** keep the board stocked: someone would love some forage, something hot, a parcel from the post office, a lost thing out on the trail, an hour helping at a shop.
- **Three ways they reach you:** the notice board in the square; asked in person after someone's line ("I'll see what I can do" or "not now"); and letters in the new mailbox by the station door.
- **Every favor gives friendship** with whoever asked.

## Things to test (the 4a checklist)

- [ ] Walk into town on the first morning. Does it feel like a different, welcoming place?
- [ ] Learn where someone will be at a given hour, and find them there.
- [ ] Hear a townsperson mention something you did in the park.
- [ ] Give a loved gift, and enjoy the reaction.
- [ ] Buy a coat, and feel the difference on a cold trip.
- [ ] Do a favor. Does the friendship reward feel worth it?
- [ ] Spend a whole week in town. Was it satisfying?
- [ ] Knock at a house. Does each feel like its owner's?

Tip: the title screen's Playtest start skips the opening jobs and starts with the road to town open.
