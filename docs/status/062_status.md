# Strange Ranger 0.6.2: the shops

From the lab, 22 November 2026. Phase 4, 4a step 5 (Shops), and the interior art request you asked for once your Stardew pictures came.

## What's in

**Walk into the shops.** Press X at a shop's door during its hours and you go inside. Out of hours the door says when it opens. At closing time the keeper sees you out. Seven shops have insides: the Percolator, Casa Luna, Humdrum Outfitters, Gifts & Notions, Lindqvist Carpentry, the library, and the Town Hall and post office.

**The keeper is behind the counter.** Keepers are no longer at their doors in the street; they're inside. Townsfolk whose day takes them to a shop are inside too, at a table or by a shelf, and you can talk to them and give them gifts as in the street.

**X at the counter** opens the shop's menu:

- **Talk to** the keeper (counts as the day's talk).
- **Buy:** each line says what it does and its price. The wallet shows in the title.
- **Sell** (Gifts & Notions only): the gift goods you make and berry preserve at half their kiosk price, plus some forage.
- **Order from the supply catalog** (post office): arrives at the station next morning, as from the desk at home.

**What's on sale:**

| Shop | Sells |
| --- | --- |
| The Percolator | Coffee 5g (walk 10% faster for two hours), hot chocolate 5g (+20 warmth), muffin of the day 6g (+40 energy, a flavour for each season), trail breakfast 12g (+90 energy, before 10:00 only) |
| Casa Luna | Tamales 18g, pozole 20g, enchiladas 20g, churros 8g (sometimes Rosa gives you a second plate). Eaten at a table, half an hour; to take away for 2g more |
| Outfitters | Rope, trail mix, firewood. Clothing comes next (step 6) |
| Gifts & Notions | Pressed flower cards, resin candles; buys what you make |
| Carpentry | Planks, roofing and nails, at catalog prices but the same day |
| Library | Ten books on the shelves. Four teach a recipe the first time you read them |
| Town Hall | The post office counter takes catalog orders |

At the Percolator and Casa Luna each dish has two lines: **here** (eaten at once) or **to take away** (into the backpack, eaten later at full effect).

## Choices the lab made

- **The honour jar.** Wren leaves the Percolator at 15:00 for the creek, but it's open till 18:00. While she's out, a note on the counter says "Leave the money in the jar": you can still buy, but not talk or sell. The same goes for any keeper who steps out.
- **Keepers inside, not at the door.** Once a shop has an inside, its keeper is in it. You find Wren behind her counter, not on the step.
- **The library's reading room** and **sitting at a Percolator table** don't restore energy yet. They will with the next pass.
- **Outfitters** sells rope, trail mix and firewood until the clothing comes, since there's no lantern oil or matches in the game.
- **Houses** still say who lives there; their insides come with the next pass.
