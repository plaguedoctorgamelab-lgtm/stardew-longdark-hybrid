# Strange Ranger: art audit

Made by `tools/art_audit.py` from every finished picture in `art/final` (Graphics Polish request, part two). Measured: stray pixels, colours used, outline colour, size against the manifest's target, and the contrast left under the night tint. The rest of the request's twelve problems (wrong view, wrong light, visible repeats, wobbly frames, clashing pieces) need an eye in a test scene and are not scored here.

**Severity:** 1 barely noticeable, 2 noticeable, 3 stands out in play. **Ranked** by severity times how often the picture is on screen (3 for the ranger, ground and trees, 2 for items, furniture and buildings, 1 for the rest).

476 pictures checked; 348 have something to look at. 15 more are pictures of things not found yet, counted only.

Contact sheet of the ten: `docs/art/art_audit_top10.png` (each alone at game size, enlarged four times, and under the night tint).
