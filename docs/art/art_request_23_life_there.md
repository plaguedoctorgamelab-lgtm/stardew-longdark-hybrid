# Strange Ranger: Art Request 23, the last pieces for "a life there"

From the lab, 23 November 2026. Phase 4b is built: heart events, romance, Esme's readings, the four festivals, the station's upgrades and decorating, and the garden. **Almost every picture comes from your Art Request 21, Part B sheets.** This request covers the few things 4b still borrows or draws in code.

**Nothing waits on these.** Each piece has a stand-in in the game today, and your picture replaces it with no code change.

**About spoilers.** Section C is a list of keepsakes the townsfolk give you in their stories. The descriptions are only what's needed to draw each one, but if you'd rather meet them in the game first, skip section C. The stand-ins work fine.

## Rules

The same as Art Requests 17 to 22: the bright palette, a one-pixel dark outline, light from the top left, big drafts are fine, cells on an even grid, each piece standing on the bottom middle of its cell, and your colours kept as drawn.

**Style line, at the start of every prompt:**

> True pixel art, crisp hard-edged pixels on an exact grid, every pixel a solid square, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, warm cozy 16-bit farming game style, bright cheerful palette, light from the top left.
