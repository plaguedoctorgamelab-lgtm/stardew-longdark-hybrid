# Strange Ranger: Art Request 15, Cursors

From the lab, 18 November 2026. The game now has its own mouse pointer (tracker item #8). Over forage the ranger can reach, the pointer shows a plus, and a click picks it up. Both pointers are lab stand-ins today (`art/placeholder/cursor_arrow.png` and `cursor_pick.png`), so nothing waits on this.

Same rules as Art Requests 11 to 14: pixel art, the bright palette of Art Request 06, a one-pixel dark outline.

| # | File | Size | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `ar15_cursors.png` | 48 × 24 | Two pointers side by side, 24 × 24 each |

- **Cell 1, the pointer:** an arrow pointing up and left, cream with a walnut edge. Its tip must be the **top-left pixel** of the cell: that's where the click lands.
- **Cell 2, the forage pointer:** the same arrow, smaller, with a green plus (or a small open hand) below and to the right. Its tip is also the top-left pixel.

They're drawn at 24 × 24 and shown at that size on screen, so keep them bold: they'll look small next to the game, which is scaled up three times.

**Prompt.** Start with the style line from Art Request 11, then:

> Two 24 by 24 pixel art mouse cursors side by side on a transparent background, one-pixel dark walnut outline: a cream arrow pointing up and to the left with its tip in the very top-left pixel; the same arrow smaller with a bright green plus sign below and to its right.
