# Strange Ranger: Phase Roadmap

Strange Ranger is built in phases, each one playable and tested before the next begins. Only phase one has a full build spec so far.

## The phases

Seven phases build year one, and each later region is a phase of its own. Each phase depends on all the ones before it.

| Phase | Name | What it adds | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bedrock | Ranger movement, clock and calendar, day and night light, weather, energy and warmth, a backpack, one repairable cabin, sleep and save, the map, a small test area | A player can hike out, get caught by weather, repair the cabin, sleep there and walk home with a partly filled map |
| 2 | Trail work | The full tool kit, gathering, crafting with the magic slot, clearing and building trail, signs, the site-opening steps, the skills Trailcraft, Surveying, Woodcraft and Foraging, fire towers and outlooks | A player can take one site from found to open |
| 3 | The park runs | Money, visitors as characters on opened ground, fees, tours, permits and rentals, set-place facilities and free placement, bathrooms and trash, the suggestion box, Chief messages, troll badger mischief, hired staff | An opened site earns income and neglect has funny, harmless results |
| 4 | Humdrum | The town, shops, townsfolk with routines, friendship, quests and romance, the fortune teller, festivals, the station as an upgradeable home with a garden | A player can spend a satisfying week without leaving town |
| 5 | Creatures and night | Animals and their strange cousins, the Lore skill, camera and field guide, creature answers, the secret, hint or public choice, night magic, aurora nights, calendar events, a pet | A player can meet, settle and decide the fate of one magical creature |
| 6 | The whole forest | The full forest and Skull Lake map, all twelve sites, three mysteries, seasons that change the land, canoe, cart, furnishable cabins, badges, the yearly review, park status, hidden things | Year one can be played from first day to first review |
| 7 | Year one polish | Balance, final art, sound and music, the opening minutes, playtest fixes | The designer would hand year one to a stranger |
| 8 to 11 | One region each | Swamp, plains, foothills and mountains, each with its palette, creatures, sites, mysteries and a year of things to do | Each region fills its year |

The order of the four later regions is not yet decided. The art for each phase is delivered as a batch of prompts when that phase's spec is written.

## How a phase runs

Every phase follows the same six steps, and the next phase's spec is not written until the current one is signed off.

1. **Spec.** The designer and Claude write the phase's build spec from the bible, the open questions list and what the last phase taught.
2. **Art.** The art prompts for the phase are written, and the designer makes the images. The lab does not wait for them.
3. **Build.** The lab builds to the spec with placeholder art, in small tested steps, and emails status.
4. **Test.** The lab runs the phase's acceptance tests and reports real results.
5. **Play.** The designer plays the build and writes down what feels wrong.
6. **Sign off.** Tuning changes are made, lessons are added to the open questions list, any change of mind is written into the bible, and the phase is closed.

A phase may be split in two if it proves too large. Nothing from a later phase is pulled forward into an earlier one.
